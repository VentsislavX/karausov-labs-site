Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to support@karausovlabs.com. We read every message.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game have ads or tracking?

No. This version has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it asks for no permissions. Your progress stays on your device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my progress?

The game has no account, so your progress lives only in a save file on your device. To start over, delete the game (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App > Delete App) and install it again. This removes all coins, upgrades, skins, stars and settings and cannot be undone. If you later restore your iPhone from a backup made earlier, the old progress may come back with it.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

There is no cloud save. Moving to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup usually brings the game's save file along with your other app data.

Does the game need the internet?

No. The whole game works offline.

How do I contact you?

Email support@karausovlabs.com. Please tell us your device model, iOS version and the game version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen). We usually answer within a few days.
